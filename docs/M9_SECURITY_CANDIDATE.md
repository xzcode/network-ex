## M9d ICE recovery (2026-11-26)
Implemented bounded retention of the authenticated ICE generation on Pion Disconnected. Stop direct sends and expose reconnecting immediately, leaving WSS relay available; retain selected candidate sockets and authenticated keepalive probes until Pion Failed/Closed (4-second disconnected threshold, additional 24-second failed threshold), signal/lease expiry, revocation, or parent cancellation. Resume direct only after a nominated pair is continuously connected for two seconds. Monitor and callback state are synchronized; retired generations cannot republish. Direct selection/send checks both the active context and absolute signed lease deadline; inbound delivery checks expiry and context. Reset retry delay after loss of a previously selected generation; preserve the prior bounded initial retry policy. No new signal protocol, crypto, NAT rules or external services.
